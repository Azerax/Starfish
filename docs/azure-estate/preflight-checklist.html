Pre-Implementation Checklist

Everything to gather, decide and prove before drawing a single container. Most of it is the client's to answer — which is exactly why it belongs at the front of an engagement. It ends in one signed artefact: the folder-to-container mapping, approved by name and recorded as a governance decision.

Items marked blocking stop the design, not just delay it — a wrong answer to any of them means redrawing rather than adjusting. Items marked by test cannot be answered from documentation; the docs are either silent or contradict themselves, so they need an hour in a real tenant.
