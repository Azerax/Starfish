Container Canvas

A container is one audience. Inside it, each agent pairs a model with its own slice of the company drive. Two paths in the same hierarchy always need their own container — inside one, they would share an identity and stop being a boundary at all.

What this would deploy 






Each agent's slice becomes a blob knowledge source with a folderPath, provisioning its own data source, indexer, skillset and index. A folder path is a recursive prefix — /marketing/*.* and /marketing/ both mean everything under marketing, and there is no way to index one folder without its subfolders. Sibling folders under one parent are disjoint and may share a container; only a containing path forces a split.
